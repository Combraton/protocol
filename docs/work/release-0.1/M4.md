## Outcome

Two profiles any provider can implement, and callers and executors can rely on:
- `evidence/1`: exact artifact identity, staged upload and seal, provenance and coverage, authorized query and fetch, manifests, availability and retention with proof-loss reports, and work bindings;
- `context/1`: requests with basis, obligations, reliance and limits; shared jobs with separate subscriber cancellation; exact packets with citations, labels, coverage and gaps; updates, corrections and stale-basis detection; and behavior when the provider is unavailable.

Conformance must distinguish honest from optimistic providers with **reference participants only**: a reference evidence provider with a scripted store, a reference context provider with scripted preparation, and the M3 reference executor. **M4 does not implement the real CBR memory engine or PIO adapters**; their evidence belongs to their own gates.

## Owner decisions (2026-09-15)

| # | Decision | Where |
|---|---|---|
| M4-Q1 | Bounded in-band chunks and ranged fetch for 0.1, with base64 and envelope overhead in the frame limit; interrupted-upload recovery and duplicate or conflicting chunks defined explicitly; locators informational with no implicit credential-bearing fetch; out-of-band transfer deferred | EVIDENCE §3, §4 |
| M4-Q2 | Each packet revision maps immutably to one exact sealed Evidence artifact; Evidence identity, integrity, retention and fetch are reused; a protocol dependency, not another running service; excerpts and omission markers never carry the artifact's digest | CONTEXT §1, §2, §5, §6 |
| M4-Q3 | Separate reference participants over public protocol connections with isolated state; a shared executable is acceptable; one grant per audience, bound to the reader and exact object; a reference grants nothing | CONTEXT §7, §12; EVIDENCE §8 |
| M4-Q4 | The executor checks typed, observable conditions only; `match`, `mismatch` and `unavailable` are distinct, and unavailable is unknown; effect by obligation; revalidation at every relevant boundary; checks recorded without rewriting packets; a negotiated, versioned Execution extension with compatibility fixtures | EXECUTION §13.1; CONTEXT §8; CMP-8 |
| M4-Q5 | New Evidence error codes, with `artifact_digest_mismatch` naming a reference mismatch; each code's ordering, details, state change and retry defined; `upload_incomplete` is `after_reconcile`, not `same_command`, with a partial-upload → append → seal fixture | EVIDENCE §6; CORE §12 |
| M4-Q6 | Purge refused while active holds remain; naming holds is intent only; purge authority plus release authority for every named hold; hold ownership and delegated release; hold revisions checked; consistent commit; tombstones, durable proof-loss records, requested versus confirmed deletion; loss reports respect reader authorization and declare coverage | EVIDENCE §9 |
| M4-Q7 | Versioned Protocol manifest format now, in-toto export later; children name exact artifact and digest; completeness per reader authorization, with unverified distinct from known missing | EVIDENCE §7 |

**Small additions made while incorporating these:**
- **Error codes:** `upload_offset_mismatch` and `upload_size_exceeded`, needed for the explicit chunk rules M4-Q1 asks for.
- **Operation:** `evidence.upload.abandon`, because received bytes are never overwritten, so a digest-mismatched upload can only be abandoned.

**Clarifications made while implementing step 2** (applications of the decisions above, recorded in EVIDENCE):
- **Query `filtered`** is `true` whenever the reader's authorization does not cover every artifact, not only when an unreadable artifact matched. Otherwise a digest filter would be an existence test, which M4-Q5 forbids.
- **Operations without their target state** (append or abandon without a staged upload, seal of an abandoned artifact, fetch of an unsealed one) are `not_found` to an authorized caller, following Execution's precedent for answered actions; `evidence.inspect` still reports the artifact.
- **Repeated purge** of an artifact already `purge_pending` or `purged` returns its availability at the unchanged revision, like a repeated seal.
- **Not available:** fetch returns the availability and no bytes; inspect and query report availability as observed at the read, including integrity failures, without recording an event.
- **Chunk limit** is bounded by the string, payload and frame limits together.
- **Producer principal:** a descriptor may repeat the session principal; naming another is refused.

## Acceptance

None may be dropped silently. If one cannot be met, it is escalated with evidence and recorded as deferred.

1. **EXE-21 is explicit.** Execution outcomes reference sealed artifacts by artifact and exact digest; references resolve only through authorized Evidence operations; mutants for unsealed or mismatched references are killed.
2. **Exact artifact and packet identity.** References carry digests; fetched bytes equal the sealed bytes; locators are never identity; regenerated or relabeled bytes are detected.
3. **Authorized access.** No existence leaks in inspect, query, fetch or expand; packet-bound read grants cover only their packet and audience.
4. **Provenance and availability.** Producer bound to the session principal; required coverage; availability states; purge with proof-loss report; integrity failure never serves bytes.
5. **Missing required context.** Unmet at deadline, never satisfied or downgraded; advisory follows its declared fallback; bound work is not admitted as ready.
6. **Corrections during preparation.** An older derivation never becomes current for a corrected binding item.
7. **Stale basis.** Detected before admission; work bound to a stale packet is not admitted as ready.
8. **Shared-subscriber cancellation.** Cancelling one request never cancels a job other requests need.
9. **CBR outage.** Advisory proceeds with a visible gap; required waits at its named boundary; no fail-open.
10. **Preparation/resource dependency cycle.** A consumer waiting for context holds no execution reservation; job-initiated executions carry their origin and are not re-enriched.
11. **Reference participants only.** No fixture depends on the real CBR memory engine or on PIO adapters; scripted providers are test environment, never protocol.
12. **Independent evidence.** The independent implementation passes the fixtures for what it claims, or its divergences are resolved.
13. **CI.** Green, with manifests, transcripts and mutant summaries uploaded and outcomes kept distinct; coverage limits are recorded, never counted as passes.
